Privacy Policy

Dash Drive  ·  Last updated October 2, 2026

The short version

Dash Drive is built so that your data stays with you. The app has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything the app records is stored on your iPhone and, if you have iCloud enabled, in your personal iCloud account. We cannot see it.

What the app collects and why

  • Location. While a trip is actively tracking, the app uses GPS to measure the distance you drive. The app asks for “Always” location access so a trip started from CarPlay or Siri can keep recording while your phone is locked. Location is only used during an active trip. It is stored as a trip distance and duration on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Calendar. With your permission, the app reads your calendar events so you can start a drive tagged to an event, and writes trip details into an event's notes if you choose. Calendar data stays in your calendar and on your device.
  • Trips, categories, and rates. The trip log you build, along with your custom categories and deduction rates, is stored on your device.
  • Settings. Preferences such as distance unit and default category are stored on your device.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, trips, rate rules, and calendar schedule rules sync to the private CloudKit database attached to your Apple Account. This is what restores your data when you install the app on a new device. The private database is controlled by Apple and accessible only to you. We have no access to it. You can turn this off by disabling iCloud Drive for Dash Drive in your iPhone's iCloud settings.

Subscriptions and payments

Dash Drive is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the purchase and handles your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation from Apple that a subscription is active, which it uses to unlock features. We never receive your name, email address, or payment information from a purchase. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings.

Exports

When you export a CSV report, the file is generated on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice.

Third-party apps

The Directions button can open Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze with a destination address from your calendar event. Once you leave Dash Drive, that app's own privacy policy applies.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate servers that receive your data.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, or advertising networks.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent any information about you.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Dash Drive is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
