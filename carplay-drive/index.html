CarPlay Drive

Smart mileage tracking, right from your car's screen.

For iPhone with CarPlay  ·  Subscription with 1-week free trial

Every deductible mile, captured

CarPlay Drive turns your car's screen into a mileage logger. Tap a category — Business, Medical, Charity, or your own — and your trip records automatically: distance, time, and the estimated deduction at your configured rate.

When you arrive, tap Complete. The trip is saved on your iPhone and, if you choose, written into the notes of the calendar event you drove to — so your mileage log and your meeting history tell the same story.

Frequently asked questions

How does trip tracking work?

Start a trip from CarPlay, your iPhone, or Siri ("Start tracking with CarPlay Drive"). The app uses GPS to measure distance while you drive. Tap Complete when you arrive and the trip is saved with its category, duration, and estimated deduction.

Why does the app ask for "Always" location access?

So a trip you start from CarPlay or Siri can keep recording while your phone stays locked in your pocket. Location is only used while a trip is actively tracking, and it never leaves your device.

What happens if I unplug from CarPlay mid-trip?

Your choice, in Settings: pause the trip quietly, complete and save it, or pause and send a notification so you can decide from your phone.

Can it skip short trips?

Yes — set a minimum trip distance in Settings and anything shorter is discarded automatically.

What does the subscription include?

Everything: CarPlay tracking, calendar integration, custom categories and rates, and CSV export. Monthly and yearly plans both start with a 1-week free trial, and you can cancel anytime in your Apple account settings.

How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?

Install the app, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings. Your trips restore automatically from your personal iCloud.
